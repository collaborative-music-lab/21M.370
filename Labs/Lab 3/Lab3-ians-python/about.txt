Arpy v1.0

Arpeggio player / chord instrument
- inspired by Orchid & other chord instruments

Inputs (based on Knobby):
- 4 pots: pins [1,2,3,4]
- 4 buttons: pins :[6,7,8,9]

Basic controls:
- buttons control root note of chord
    - roots are defined in button_root = [0,3,4,5]
- pots:
    1: chord_size: number of notes in chord
    2: chord_voicing: lowest note of chord voicing
    3: synth timbre (cutoff and env length)
    4: root of 4th button, e.g. button_root[3]
